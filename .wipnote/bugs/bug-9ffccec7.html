Application phase cards show hard-coded durations instead of measured elapsed time

Todo Medium Priority

Part Of:

  • trk-5d3b688f

Description

ApplicationDetail renders phaseDuration() constants of 1.4s, 3.8s, and 12.1s for completed phases. This makes slow runs look arbitrary and conflicts with the dashboard, which already computes average apply time from started_at and finished_at. Durable fix: remove phaseDuration constants and compute real run/phase durations from apply_runs timestamps and transcript phase_boundary/phase_complete timestamps, or expose per-phase timings from the API; test that displayed durations come from fixture timestamps.
