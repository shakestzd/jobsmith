Factcheck gate: verify acronym claims against their spelled-out expansion (initialism match)

Todo High Priority

Part Of:

  • trk-45e7fcd5

Description

Acronym proper-noun claims (MIT, IRA, PJM) false-fail when the source spells out the full name but not the acronym (e.g. master has 'Massachusetts Institute of Technology' but resume says 'MIT'). Add an initialism-match fallback in factcheck.py: for an all-caps acronym claim not found literally, verify it against a sequence of capitalized words in any source whose leading letters spell the acronym (skipping lowercase connectors of/the/and/for). Ends recurring acronym whack-a-mole. Must not pass a fabricated acronym whose expansion is absent.
